# Privacy

What stays on your Mac

## What stays on your Mac

Habitat is designed so that everyday tool data stays local. That includes clipboard history, disk scans, battery metrics, connection lists, window switcher state, and similar app data generated while you use Habitat.

## No accounts or product telemetry

There are no user accounts inside Habitat and no product telemetry pipeline that uploads your personal files, clipboard contents, or usage analytics for advertising or profiling.

## When Habitat talks to the internet

Network activity is limited to things you explicitly trigger or that are required for core product functions:

- License purchase, activation, validation, or deactivation through [Dodo Payments](https://dodo.pe/hatitat-pro)
- Sparkle update checks and fetching update metadata / release assets
- Optional outbound links you open yourself, such as GitHub releases or Buy Me a Coffee

## Permissions

Some tools need macOS permissions such as Accessibility or Screen Recording to work as intended. Those permissions are requested by the system and controlled in **System Settings → Privacy & Security**. Habitat uses them to provide local features—not to export your private data.

## Purchases and licenses

Habitat Pro is a one-time purchase processed by Dodo Payments. Payment details are handled by that provider according to their own privacy practices. Habitat receives what it needs to activate and manage your license key on your Mac.

## Website

This website is a static marketing and download site. It does not require an account. Third-party services used for hosting, fonts, icons, or payment checkout may process standard technical data such as IP address and browser information under their own policies.
